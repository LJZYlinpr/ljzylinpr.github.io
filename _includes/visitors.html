About these statistics

Location is approximate and based on your network IP, not GPS. Your full IP is shown only to you and is not stored in the statistics database. Unique visitors are estimated from a keyed hash of the network IP; shared networks and VPNs affect this estimate. Only aggregate country counts are public. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control requests are excluded from counting.
